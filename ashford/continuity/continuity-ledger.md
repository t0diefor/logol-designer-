## ⚠ THE BREATH LOG — the most important table in this project

The Cold is canon as of 2026-11-02. Breath fogging is now a factual statement
about whether a character is producing body heat. **One wrong panel undoes the
whole device.** Every page in cold air gets a row here before it is inked.

| Issue | Page | Panel | Character | Breath fogs? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| #1 | 1 | 2 | Roz | **YES** | alive, human-warm, has not used the power in this panel |
| #1 | 1 | 4 | Roz | **YES** | the contrast the page exists for |
| #1 | 1 | 4 | attacker | **NO** | the reader's first horror beat |
| #1 | 1 | 5 | Roz | **YES, thinning to nothing** | the last one |
| #1 | 2 | — | attacker | **NO** | never, on any page, ever |
| #1 | 3 | — | Evan | **YES** | **baseline panel.** He has not used the power yet. Without this the reader has nothing to notice the change against — do not skip it |
| #1 | 14–16 | — | Evan | **to be decided** | depends on Q13 (how long the Cold lasts) and Q14 (does he notice) |

**Standing rules:**
- The attacker's breath never fogs. Anywhere. Ever.
- Evan fogs normally until the first time he uses the power on the page.
- Once Q13 is answered, fill in the 14–16 rows before scripting them.
