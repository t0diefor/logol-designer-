# Image prompts — Issue #1

**These are production aids, not guarantees.** Image generators do not hold a
character design steady between runs. Treat output as rough reference for
composition, lighting and mood — never as finished art, and never as the
source of truth for how a character looks. The source of truth is
`characters/`.

**Do not generate Julian.** No reference has been supplied; see
`characters/reference-status.md`.

Each prompt below follows the same order: identity → action → environment →
composition → lighting → continuity.

---

### Page 1, Panel 1 — establishing
> A lone figure in work coveralls walking away from camera along a wet concrete
> breakwater at night, seen from behind and far away, small in frame. Working
> fish plant with corrugated metal siding and lit windows behind and to the
> left. Heavy sea fog. Sodium vapour lamps at intervals, each a narrow orange
> cone that does not reach far. Steady rain visible against the lights.
> Wide establishing shot, horizon low, deep perspective down the breakwater.
> Cold blue-grey palette with sodium orange the only warm colour. Pacific
> Northwest coastal town, autumn, late night. Comic book line art with flat
> colour, not photographic.

### Page 1, Panel 2 — Roz
> A 24-year-old woman in fish-plant coveralls with sleeves pushed up, hair
> pulled back and soaked flat, standing still on a wet breakwater at night,
> holding a two-foot length of rebar loosely at her side like a tool rather
> than a weapon. Head turned slightly, chin up, listening rather than looking.
> Relaxed shoulders, unafraid. Visible breath fogging in cold air.
> Medium shot, slightly below eye level. Fog, rain, orange sodium light from
> above and behind. Cold palette. Comic book style.

### Page 1, Panel 4 — the detail the page exists for
> Tight horizontal two-shot, side on, of a young woman and a pale ordinary-
> looking figure facing each other at close distance on a rain-soaked dock at
> night, the figure's hand gripping her forearm. Close enough to see both
> mouths and the air between them. **Her breath fogs visibly in the cold air;
> the figure's does not.** The figure looks unremarkable, not monstrous, face
> partly in shadow. Her expression is flat recognition, not terror.
> Narrow horizontal composition. Low contrast, sodium light, fog. Quiet and
> still. Comic book style.

### Page 1, Panel 5 — the last breath
> Low angle, camera near deck level, on a wet concrete breakwater at night. A
> young woman in coveralls lies on her side in the foreground, eyes open, a
> length of rebar rolled away against the lip of the breakwater. Rain pocking
> puddles. The fog behind her is empty — nobody there. A faint thin plume of
> breath rising from her mouth and dissipating.
> Wide, largest panel on the page. Sodium orange rim light, deep blue-grey
> shadow. Rain. Comic book style, flat colour.

### Prompt-writing notes for later pages
- Always name the cap ("backwards black and white cap") for Evan and the red
  varsity jacket for Liam — they are the two strongest identity cues and
  generators drop them first.
- Always state the weather and time of day; it is the fastest way a generated
  panel breaks continuity.
- Never ask a generator for two blond teenage boys in one image until the
  design differentiation in `characters/README.md` is settled. It will produce
  the same boy twice.
