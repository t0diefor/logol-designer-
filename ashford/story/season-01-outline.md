# Season 1 — shape only

**Deliberately thin.** Mikey asked not to have a season drafted before the
direction is established, and that is the right instinct: a detailed season
outline written now would lock in choices that Issue #1 has not earned yet.
This file records only the two things Season 1 must deliver, both of which are
already canon.

## Fixed points [CANON]
1. **Issue #1:** the previous Slayer dies; Evan inherits; he does not know
   vampires exist at the start and does by the end.
2. **Later in Season 1:** Liam learns the truth during a rescue, and
   *initially remains a civilian*.

Everything between and around those is open.

## The one structural question worth answering early
**How many issues until Liam finds out?**

The whole season's rhythm depends on it, because the secret is the engine. Two
workable answers:

- **Mid-season (around issue 4 of 8).** The lie has time to be established and
  half a season to have consequences. Recommended — it avoids a long stretch
  where the reader is waiting for an inevitable reveal.
- **Season finale.** More traditional, and more dangerous: the reader will see
  it coming for six issues, and the "Evan almost tells him" scene can only be
  played so many times before it reads as stalling.

Not a question for today. Noted here so it is not decided by accident.

## Workload reality
At one page at a time, largely alone, an 8-issue season at 22 pages is 176
pages. That is a multi-year commitment and is how most first comics die.

**Recommendation:** make Season 1 **six issues of sixteen pages** — 96 pages.
Still substantial, and each issue is a finishable object. Issue #1's outline is
built at 16 pages for this reason.
