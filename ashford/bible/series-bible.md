## 1. Confirmed canon

**Title.** Ashford. [CANON]

**Setting.** A fictional coastal British Columbia town. Rain, fog, forest,
a waterfront. Pacific Northwest gothic. [CANON]

**Genre.** Supernatural teen drama: vampire horror, humour, mystery, action,
and a slow-burn gay romance. [CANON]

**Premise.** Evan Mercer, 17, gay, human — not a vampire — becomes the new
Slayer when the previous Slayer dies in Issue #1. He starts out not knowing
vampires exist. The reader discovers alongside him. [CANON]

**Powers.** Supernatural strength, reflexes, and other classic Slayer
abilities. [CANON]

**The cost — the Cold.** Using the power stops Evan producing body heat. His
breath does not fog, his hands go cold, animals avoid him after heavy use, and
recovery is slow and physical. [CANON, 2026-11-02] — full rule in
`world-rules.md`. Duration and recovery curve remain [OPEN], question Q13.

**Watcher.** Dr. Julian Blackwood is Evan's assigned Watcher. Serious,
sophisticated, knowledgeable about the occult. He initially serves a secret
Watcher organization. In Season 3 he leaves it to protect Evan from a
potentially fatal Slayer test ordered by that organization. [CANON]

**Romance.** Evan and Liam are not boyfriends at the start. Liam begins as a
civilian who knows nothing. He learns the truth during a rescue later in
Season 1 and initially stays a civilian. The romance develops slowly, and
stays age-appropriate and non-explicit. [CANON]

**Antagonist.** Seraphine, a vampire queen. Appears about 30, is over 200.
Black hair, tailored suits, a silver ring on her right hand. [CANON]

**Also established.** Dr. Elias Thorne — a separate person from Julian —
clinical-looking, gray temples, a scar on his left hand. Supporting cast:
Inspector Kane (police investigator), Madame Odelia (rare book shop owner),
and a conflicted vampire who appears about 20. [CANON that they exist; their
details are [OPEN]]

**Locations.** Ashford High School, Main Street, the Waterfront District,
Cliffs Overlook, the Rare Book Shop, the Old Quarter (vampire territory).
[CANON] — see `locations.md`.

## 2. The story engine

> **One town. Two boys. One secret.**
> The romance and the horror plot run on the same fuel.

This is the series' real strength and it is worth protecting above everything
else in this document.

Evan's calling demands concealment. The romance demands disclosure. Ashford is
small enough that he cannot avoid Liam, cannot patrol without being seen, and
cannot lie at distance — he has to lie to Liam's face, repeatedly, while
wanting the opposite. Every scene that advances the romance therefore strains
the secret, and every scene that advances the horror plot makes the secret
heavier. Nothing has to be invented to keep those two engines turning. They
turn each other.

Practical consequence for scripting: **never resolve a horror beat in a place
Liam cannot plausibly turn up.** Proximity is the engine. Keep them close.

## 3. Tone

Wet, cold, funny, and quiet before it is loud.

Ashford is a working town, not a spooky one — a fish plant, a ferry terminal,
a high school with a leaking roof. The horror is better when the town is
ordinary. The jokes should come from character and exhaustion rather than from
quips at the monster; a teenager who is frightened and tired is funny in a way
that a teenager performing cleverness is not.

Violence has weight. Evan gets hurt and stays hurt — injuries are tracked in
`continuity/continuity-ledger.md` and are expected to show up on later pages.

## 4. What Ashford is deliberately *not*

The Slayer/Watcher framework is recognisable. These notes exist so the series
does not drift further toward its influences by accident. See
`decisions/proposals.md` for the specific alternatives proposed.

- Not a global chosen-one story. [PROPOSAL: the power is local to Ashford.]
- Not an ancient robed council. [PROPOSAL: the organization is a mundane
  coastal bureaucracy.]
- Not a monster-of-the-week structure with a reset at the end of each issue.
  Consequences carry.
- Not a story where the powers solve the problem. The powers are what make the
  problem personal — and since 2026-11-02 they have a price that is paid in
  Evan's body and shows on the page.
