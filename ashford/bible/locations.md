# Ashford — locations

Confirmed locations are canon. The texture notes under each are [PROPOSAL]
unless marked otherwise, and exist so that artists and scripts describe the
same place twice.

**Geography rule:** Ashford is small enough to cross on foot in forty minutes.
That is a story constraint, not just a detail — it is why Evan cannot avoid
anyone, which is the engine of the series. Do not let the town grow.

---

### Ashford High School [CANON]
Low 1970s build, flat roof that leaks, portables out back that have been
"temporary" for fifteen years. Field drains badly. Smells like wet carpet from
October to April.
*Used for:* Evan and Liam's ordinary life. The baseline the horror ruins.

### Main Street [CANON]
Six blocks. Hardware store, bakery, the laundromat, two coffee places that
hate each other. Half the shopfronts are seasonal and dark in winter.
*Used for:* being seen. Nothing happens on Main Street that stays private.

### The Waterfront District [CANON]
Working waterfront, not a boardwalk — fish plant, net lofts, a ferry terminal,
a breakwater, sodium lights in the fog. Shift change at 11pm puts real people
in the dark at the exact hour the story wants them there.
*Used for:* Issue #1 page 1. The series' signature location.

### Cliffs Overlook [CANON]
Above the inlet, reached by a switchback trail. Where kids go. Wind, no cover,
a long drop, and the only place in Ashford with a view of the whole town.
*Used for:* conversations that could not happen anywhere private enough.
Earmarked for a romance beat; keep it unused until it is earned.

### The Rare Book Shop [CANON]
Madame Odelia's. Narrow, over-stocked, a bell on the door.
*Used for:* research and exposition. **Script discipline:** this is the room
where exposition is easiest and therefore most dangerous. Prefer to break
exposition across two visits rather than deliver it in one.

### The Old Quarter [CANON — vampire territory]
The oldest blocks, above the original wharf. Brick, subsidence, alleys at odd
angles because they follow the shoreline as it was in 1890.
*Used for:* enemy ground. Evan going there should always feel like a decision.
