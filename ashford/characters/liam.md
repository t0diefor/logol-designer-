# Liam

## Confirmed [CANON]
- 17.
- Platinum / white-blond hair, warm blue-green eyes, athletic build,
  red varsity jacket.
- Begins as a civilian who knows nothing about Evan's calling.
- Discovers the truth during a rescue later in Season 1.
- **Initially remains a civilian** after finding out.
- Not Evan's boyfriend at the start. The romance is slow.

---

## The craft note that matters most

"Civilian who finds out and stays a civilian" is an unusually good decision and
it should be defended, because the pressure to make him a sidekick will be
constant. A partner who cannot fight has to be useful some other way — he knows
people, he can be somewhere Evan cannot, he can lie to adults convincingly, he
can hold a door. And he can be genuinely, frighteningly at risk, which a
sidekick with a crossbow cannot.

**Rule for Season 1:** Liam never wins a fight. If a scene needs him to solve
something, he solves it with information, access, or nerve.

## The second craft note

Liam needs a want that is not Evan. If his entire function is to be lied to and
then to forgive, he is furniture.

**[PROPOSAL]** Liam is the only one of the two who actually likes Ashford and
intends to stay — he has a place here, people, a season coming up. That makes
him the opposite number to Evan's quiet plan to leave, gives their scenes a
subject other than the secret, and means the eventual reveal costs them both
something different. It also makes the red jacket mean something: it is a
uniform, it belongs to a team, and it is the most *rooted* object in the comic.

## Voice
Warm, direct, and not stupid. The temptation with this character is to write
him oblivious so the secret survives; resist it. Liam should notice things
early and be *put off* repeatedly — that is more interesting than a boy who
notices nothing, and it makes the eventual reveal feel overdue rather than
convenient.

He should ask Evan a direct question at least once per issue and get a
non-answer. The reader counts those.

## Appearance [CANON, 2026-11-02]

Stays platinum. Separated from Evan by silhouette and colour, not by hair
colour:

- Bare head. No cap, ever, in Season 1 — the absence is half the silhouette.
- Open shape: the varsity jacket's broad shoulders, weight forward.
- Hair warmer cream-blond with **volume and darker roots** — this is what
  carries the difference in black and white, where hue cannot help.
- Rounder face, heavier brow, broader nose.
- **The red jacket is the only strong colour on a person in Season 1.** In any
  panel with Evan, the red finds the eye first. That is the design doing the
  storytelling: Liam is rooted and visible, Evan is not.

## What the Cold gives him [CANON knock-on, 2026-11-02]

Liam is the person most likely to touch Evan casually, which makes him the
first person positioned to notice that Evan's hands are cold. That is now a
real, physical thread running under the romance from early in Season 1 — the
closer they get, the more obvious the lie becomes, without anyone writing a
scene about it.
