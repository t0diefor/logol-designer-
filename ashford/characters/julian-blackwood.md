# Dr. Julian Blackwood

- Evan's **assigned** Watcher.
- Serious, sophisticated, knowledgeable about the occult.
- Initially serves a secret Watcher organization.
- **Season 3:** leaves that organization to protect Evan from a potentially

## The word "assigned"

"Assigned" is the most useful word in Julian's canon and it should be played
hard. It means he did not choose Evan, Evan did not choose him, and both of
them know it. Their relationship starts as paperwork. Everything warm between
them later has to be earned against that, which is a much better arc than a
mentor who arrives already caring.

**Rule for Issue #1:** Julian explains nothing. He *assesses*. He asks the
questions on his form, in order, and does not answer any of Evan's. The reader
should finish Issue #1 disliking him.

## [PROPOSAL] — what the organization actually is

The most derivative element in the whole series is "secret council of Watchers."
It is also the easiest to change, because Mikey's canon never says what the
organization *looks* like — only that it is secret, assigns Watchers, and later
orders a dangerous test.

Proposal: it is not ancient, robed or academic. It is a **mundane coastal
institution with filing cabinets** — something like a maritime survey office, a
salvage and insurance body, or a lighthouse service: the kind of organisation
that has had a quiet reason to keep records of every coastal town in the
province for a hundred years, and that nobody looks at twice.

Why this is better:
- Julian carrying a **clipboard** instead of a leather tome is funnier, colder,
  and instantly not borrowed.
- Bureaucracies are frightening in a way that cabals are not. A cabal wants
  something. An office has a *procedure*, and nobody in it is responsible.
- It sets up Season 3 properly. See `decisions/proposals.md`, proposal P4, for
  a reshaping of the Season 3 test that keeps Mikey's canon event — Julian
  leaves to protect Evan — while moving away from a very recognisable existing
  scene.

Nothing here renames anything. "Watcher" and "the organization" stay. This is
only about what the room looks like. Mikey's call.

## Voice
Complete sentences. No contractions when he is being official; contractions
only when he has slipped. Never raises his voice. The first time he is
genuinely rude is a story event.
