## Note before he is used

See `README.md` — two characters titled "Dr." and both written in clinical
terms will be confused by readers. Recommended handling: he is always
"Dr. Thorne" in dialogue, Julian is always "Blackwood," and they do not share
a panel until the reader has seen each alone at least twice.

## Hooks worth considering (all [PROPOSAL], pick none or one)

- The scar is on his **left** hand, specifically, and Mikey has noted it. That
  is a detail that wants to be a clue. Something closed on it.
- "Clinical-looking" plus the title suggests medicine. Ashford has a hospital
  or a clinic that nobody has written yet, and a doctor is the one person in
  town who sees every body that comes in off the water. That is an enormous
  amount of knowledge for a supporting character to be sitting on.
- The most useful version of him for the series is probably **not** a villain
  and **not** an ally — a professional with his own reasons who will help Evan
  exactly as far as his interests go.
