# Supporting cast

All three are **[CANON]** as existing. Their details are **[OPEN]** and are
deliberately left thin until a story needs them — filling them in now would be
inventing canon Mikey has not asked for.

---

## Inspector Kane
**Canon:** a police investigator.

Useful function: the person for whom Evan's behaviour looks *exactly* like
guilt. Bodies are appearing; a 17-year-old keeps being nearby; he lies badly
about where he has been. Kane does not need to be wrong, or stupid, or
corrupt — a competent investigator drawing a reasonable, incorrect conclusion
is more pressure than a crooked cop.

If the Cold proposal is accepted, Kane is also the one person likely to notice
that Evan does not shiver.

**[OPEN]:** age, gender, rank, how long in Ashford, and whether Kane is from
here.

## Madame Odelia
**Canon:** owns the rare book shop.

Script discipline more than character: her shop is where exposition will want
to happen, which makes it the most dangerous room in the comic. Recommend she
is unhelpful at least as often as she is helpful, and that she charges — for
books, for time, for favours. A free oracle flattens every mystery.

**[OPEN]:** whether she knows what Evan is, what she is, and what she wants.

## The conflicted vampire
**Canon:** appears about 20. Conflicted.

The most familiar character type in the vampire genre, which means the version
of him that works will be the one with a specific, small, concrete conflict
rather than a general moral anguish. "He does not want to be what he is" is not
a character. "He still turns up to a job he no longer needs" is.

Worth deciding early whether he is **older than he looks** — appearing 20 and
being 20 are extremely different characters, and it changes whether he is
Evan's peer or someone pretending to be.

**[OPEN]:** name, age at death, what he wants, his relationship to Seraphine
and to the Old Quarter.
