# Seraphine

## Confirmed [CANON]
- Vampire queen.
- Appears around 30. Is over 200 years old.
- Black hair. Tailored suits. A silver ring on her right hand.

---

## The craft problem

"Centuries-old elegant vampire queen in tailored clothes" is the genre default.
The look is canon and should stay — it is a strong, drawable silhouette and it
contrasts well with a cast of teenagers in hoodies. What makes her original is
not her appearance. It is **what she wants**, and that is not established yet.

Two wants to avoid, because they are the ones the genre reaches for first:
immortal boredom, and a plan to end the world. Neither gives Evan anything to
do but punch.

## [PROPOSAL] — what Seraphine wants

She is 200 years old on a coast that was violently remade inside her lifetime.
She has watched the town be built, renamed, burned down and rebuilt. Propose
that what she wants is **continuity** — Ashford staying exactly as it is,
indefinitely, with her in it. She is not trying to take the town. She already
has it, quietly, and has had it for a century.

That makes her a conservative force, which is unusual and useful:
- The Slayer is the destabilising element, not her. Evan is the one changing
  things, which complicates who the reader roots for.
- It explains the Old Quarter: she keeps her kind contained there because an
  uncontrolled vampire population would draw attention and *change things*.
- It makes her and Julian's organization structurally similar — two
  institutions managing the coast quietly — which is a much more interesting
  antagonism than good versus evil.
- The silver ring gets a meaning: it is old, it is from a specific year, and it
  is the only thing on her that is not current.

## The thing she must not be

She should not be the obstacle in every issue. A 200-year-old who personally
handles a teenager's problems is not frightening. Most of Season 1 she should
be a **consequence** — things Evan does reach her, and she responds at a
distance, through other people.
